# Course overview – University · Computer Science {#kurseinstieg}

## Who is this course for? {#S1}

This self-study course is intended for learners with upper secondary mathematics and basic computer skills. It develops algorithmic thinking, formal modelling, programming and analysis of software and hardware systems. M1, M8, M10 and M12–M16 strengthen the Bachelor-level core; the other modules include web, databases, cloud, AI and advanced topics. M17–M20 introduce compilers, formal methods, parallel computing, graphics and research projects. Follow the prerequisites in each lesson; numbers are stable addresses rather than a mandatory semester sequence.
