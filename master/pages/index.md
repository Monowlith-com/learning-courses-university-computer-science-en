## Study and learning orientation {#S9}

M1 covers central foundations, M2–M4 servers, the cloud and distributed databases, and M5–M6 artificial intelligence and robotics. M8–M10 explore Bachelor's-level elective topics in more depth; M7 offers an introductory Master's-level core path. M11 adds elective topics. For focused further study, the source lists at the end of the respective lessons are helpful.
